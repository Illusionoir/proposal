Thank you, sweetheart! 💞

I hope what we have keeps growing stronger and sweeter. I want to be someone you can lean on anytime: to listen, celebrate your happiest moments, and stand beside you through the hard ones. You’ll always be loved, appreciated, and one of my first priorities. You are truly precious to me.

Whatever life brings, I’ll be your number-one supporter. I want to share the adventures, the quiet moments, the ups and downs—and, if we’re lucky, a whole future together. My biggest hope is to become someone you can trust and turn to, just as you’ve become a source of hope for me.

Always rooting for you ✨
